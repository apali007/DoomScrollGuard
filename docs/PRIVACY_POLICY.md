# DoomScroll Guard: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

DoomScroll Guard helps you spend less time scrolling social media feeds. It's designed so that
**none of your data ever leaves your phone**.

## What the app accesses

DoomScroll Guard uses Android's **Accessibility Service API** for one purpose: to notice when you
have been scrolling a feed app for a long time, and to show a reminder on top of that app.

Through the Accessibility Service, the app receives only:
- **The package name of the app on screen** (for example, `com.instagram.android`), so it knows
  whether you're in one of the apps you chose to watch.
- **That a scroll happened, and how far**, so it can count swipes and estimate distance scrolled.

The app is configured so that it **cannot read the content of your screen**
(`canRetrieveWindowContent="false"`). It never sees text, messages, images, videos, passwords, or
anything else displayed by other apps.

The app also reads the list of installed apps, only to let you choose which ones to watch.

## What the app stores

On your device only:
- Your settings (which apps to watch, strictness level, daily goal, etc.).
- Daily totals of time spent in watched apps.
- A log of reminders shown and what you chose (left / kept scrolling), including any reason you
  typed on the "intention" screen.

## What the app shares

**Nothing.** DoomScroll Guard does not request the Internet permission, so it is technically unable
to send data anywhere. There are no analytics, ads, trackers, or accounts.

The only time anything leaves the app is if you tap "Steps for my phone" in the battery help
section, which opens a public web page (dontkillmyapp.com) in your browser.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or go to **Settings → Apps → DoomScroll Guard → Storage → Clear storage**.
All data is deleted immediately.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
